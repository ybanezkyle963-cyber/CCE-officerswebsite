About the College

About CCE & ASCB

The College of Computer Education at Andres Soriano Colleges of Bislig, and the student council that serves it.

Our Roots

Andres Soriano Colleges of Bislig

Founded in 1952, Andres Soriano Colleges of Bislig (ASCB) has grown from a small local institution into one of Bislig City's established centers of higher education, shaping generations of graduates across its various colleges.

Within ASCB, the College of Computer Education (CCE) trains students in computing, software development, and information systems — preparing them for careers in an increasingly digital economy.

Est. 1952

College of Computer Education

Mission & vision

Mission

To provide quality, industry-relevant computing education that equips students with technical skill, ethical grounding, and the adaptability to thrive in a fast-changing digital world.

Vision

To be recognized as a leading center for computing education in the region, producing graduates who innovate responsibly and serve their communities through technology.

Core Values

What guides the college

Excellence

Holding every project, defense, and deliverable to a high standard.

Integrity

Practicing honesty in code, coursework, and conduct.

Innovation

Encouraging curiosity and creative problem-solving with technology.

Service

Using technical skill to give back to the campus and wider community.
