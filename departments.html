Academic Programs

Departments of the College

CCE organizes its curriculum into program areas, each with its own faculty coordinator and year-level sections represented by a department mayor.

BS Information Technology

Focused on applied computing — networks, systems administration, and software solutions for real-world business needs.

NetworkingWeb & Mobile DevSystems Support

BS Computer Science

A theory-and-research track covering algorithms, software engineering, and emerging fields like AI and data science.

AlgorithmsSoftware EngineeringAI & Data

BS Information Systems

Bridges technology and business — database design, systems analysis, and IT project management for organizations.

DatabasesBusiness AnalysisIT Governance

Student Development Unit

Runs orientation, mentoring, and skills training that support students outside the regular classroom.

MentoringSeminarsCareer Prep

Research & Capstone Office

Guides thesis and capstone groups from proposal to defense, and coordinates the annual research colloquium.

Thesis AdvisingColloquiumPublications

Community Extension

Organizes CCE's outreach — coding workshops and tech literacy drives for partner schools and barangays.
